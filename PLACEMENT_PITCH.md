# SchedWise — Placement Pitch

**One-line pitch:** An explainable campus scheduling platform that generates conflict-aware timetables and lets administrators compare student-first, balanced and resource-efficient scheduling priorities.

**What to show:**
1. Resource catalogue (courses, faculty, student groups and rooms).
2. Three optimization profiles and the regenerated timetable.
3. Quality insights: hard-conflict audit, compactness, seat utilization and weekday distribution.
4. Rule-based recommendations and CSV export.

**Resume title:** SchedWise — Explainable Multi-Objective Campus Timetable Optimizer

**Resume bullets:**
- Built a full-stack campus timetable optimizer using React, TypeScript, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy and PostgreSQL support, with JWT authentication and CSV export.
- Implemented MRV-based backtracking with three weighted optimization profiles to balance student idle gaps, faculty spacing and room-capacity utilization while enforcing hard constraints.
- Developed an explainable quality-audit API and dashboard reporting scheduling conflicts, compactness, campus-day footprint, seat utilization and weekday load distribution with rule-based recommendations.
- Validated the seeded 23-session demonstration workload with zero audited faculty, student-group and room-slot clashes using automated tests.

**Be ready to explain:** MRV, backtracking, hard vs soft constraints, why the quality score is heuristic, and what you would change to support multi-hour labs.
